# How I Passed the AWS Certified DevOps Professional Exam in 60 Days

## Understanding the AWS Certified DevOps Professional Exam

Before diving into my study plan, let's break down what this certification is all about and why it's so valuable for professionals in the cloud and DevOps space.

### Exam Overview

The AWS Certified DevOps Engineer Professional exam is designed for individuals who perform a DevOps engineering role. It validates a candidate's technical expertise in:

1. Provisioning, operating, and managing distributed systems and services on AWS
2. Implementing and managing continuous delivery systems and methodologies
3. Automating security controls, governance processes, and compliance validation
4. Defining and deploying monitoring, metrics, and logging systems
5. Implementing highly available, scalable, and self-healing systems
6. Designing, managing, and maintaining tools to automate operational processes

### Exam Details

- **Level**: Professional
- **Length**: 180 minutes
- **Cost**: $300 USD
- **Format**: 75 questions (multiple choice and multiple response)
- **Delivery Method**: Testing center or online proctored exam

### Prerequisites

While there are no formal prerequisites for taking this exam, I strongly recommend having:

1. At least one of the following AWS certifications:
   - AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate
   - AWS Certified Developer - Associate

2. Hands-on experience with AWS services and DevOps practices

In my case, I had previously obtained the AWS Solutions Architect Associate, AWS Developer Associate, and AWS Security Specialty certifications. This foundation, combined with my day-to-day experience as a cloud security engineer, gave me a significant advantage when preparing for the DevOps Professional exam.

## Exam Day Experience and Tips

On the day of the exam, I chose to take it at a testing center rather than online. Here are some insights from my experience:

1. **Time Management**: The 180 minutes go by quickly. Pace yourself and don't spend too much time on any single question.

2. **Question Complexity**: The questions are very detailed and often require careful reading. Practice process of elimination to narrow down your choices.

3. **Review Time**: I only had about 15 minutes at the end to review 20 out of 75 questions. Try to flag questions you're unsure about for later review.

4. **Physical Preparation**: 
   - Hydrate well before the exam
   - Eat a good meal (preferably with complex carbs for sustained energy)
   - Avoid excessive caffeine

5. **Mental Preparation**: Stay calm and confident. Remember that you've prepared well and have the knowledge to succeed.

## Lessons Learned and Advice for Future Candidates

1. **Build a Strong Foundation**: Having prior AWS certifications and hands-on experience is invaluable. If you're new to AWS, start with the associate-level certifications before attempting the DevOps Professional.

2. **Hands-on Practice is Crucial**: Don't just memorize facts. Spend time working with the AWS services in a sandbox environment.

3. **Use Multiple Resources**: Combining a comprehensive course like Stephane Maarek's with practice exams and cheat sheets provides a well-rounded preparation.

4. **Focus on DevOps Concepts**: Understand CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, monitoring, and automation deeply.

5. **Practice Time Management**: The exam is as much about managing your time as it is about knowing the content. Practice with timed mock exams.

6. **Stay Updated**: AWS services evolve rapidly. Make sure you're studying the most current information available.
